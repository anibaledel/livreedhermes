Privacy policy — See your music

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Publisher: Anibal Edelberto Amiot, author of La Livrée d'Hermès. Contact: anibaledel@gmail.com.

In one sentence

Nothing you listen to leaves your device.

What the application uses

  • The microphone, if you choose the “microphone” source: the sound is analysed live on your device to draw the patterns. It is not recorded, not sent and not kept.
  • An audio file that you choose: it is played on your device only. It is not uploaded.
  • Local storage: the application may remember your settings on the device (ground, rhythm, palette, warning already read). This information stays on the device and you can erase it by deleting the application's data.
  • The images and videos you record: they are created on your device and saved where you decide. We have no access to them.

What the application does not do

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • No tracking or behaviour-analysis tool.
  • No personal data collected, sold or shared with third parties.

Network

The application loads its own files (patterns, grounds, translations) from the website anibal-amiot.com. As with any website, the server may keep the usual technical logs (IP address, date, requested file) for its security and proper operation. They are not used to identify you.

On the web page “See your music”, the “La semaine” block offers YouTube and TikTok videos: nothing is loaded from those services until you click play; after that click, the video is governed by the policy of the service concerned.

Purchases

If a paid version is offered, the purchase is handled by the app store (Google Play). We receive neither your payment details nor your identity: only the confirmation that the unlock has been bought.

Children

The application collects no data, including from children. It contains a warning about high-contrast images (see the photosensitivity warning in the application).

Your rights

Since no personal data is collected, there is nothing for you to access or for us to delete. For any question, write to anibaledel@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the new version is published at the same address.
