Collection animations

One fixed recipe per collection

Each ground collection has its animation: a fixed sequence of patterns from the corpus, each the closest to the one before, cross-faded. The same collection always gives the same animation. The 34 animations are arranged in six groups, and each group shows one of them here, its sample. Nothing loads before a click, and only one animation plays at a time. To compose with sound, full screen: the Wallpapers page (in French).
