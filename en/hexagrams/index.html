The 64 hexagrams

Each hexagram of the Yi Jing, in the chronological (binary-weight) order used on this site, with its judgement, image, trigrams and associated magic square.

What is a hexagram?

A Yi Jing hexagram is a figure of six lines, solid (yang) or broken (yin): there are 64, each made of two stacked trigrams. This page lists them in the site's chronological order (binary weights, 0 to 63) with the traditional King Wen number. Open one to read its judgement, image, six lines and associated magic square. To draw one at random, try the free Yi King draw.
