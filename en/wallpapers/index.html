La Livrée d'Hermès

Wallpapers

Choose a category of unified patterns, full screen, reacting to sound or in meditative mode.

La Livrée d'Hermès is a book of philosophy and mathematics, freely available at the link below. It is centered on the construction of magic squares and their transcription into Jacquard weaving.

Two tools, one below the other: the three-colour, then the two-colour. Choose a category of unified patterns; once started, the tiling fills the whole screen and changes with the rhythm of sound — yours (microphone) or a file you provide.

Three-colour

Three colours, derived from one hue. Each collection's fixed animation is in the animation gallery.

Two-colour

Two colours, ink and cream by default: the figure and the ground of each pattern, read in binary, with the collection chosen here.
