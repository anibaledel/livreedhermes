Anibal Amiot — author of the project

Anibal Amiot is the designer of the La Livrée d'Hermès project, an original piece of research on the geometric construction of self-constructed magic squares (“solar squares”) and their application to the Yi King draw. Based in Thailand, he is also developing a textile side of this research, together with local players in the sector.

Two French patents filed in 2002 and 2004 (FR2840678, FR2865054), and 63 designs registered on July 4, 2019 (registration no. 20193057) covering the principal order-12 squares — the geometric foundation of La Livrée d'Hermès. For the documented detail of these titles, see the full documentary profile, or the Wikidata entry for Anibal Edelberto Amiot.

Inventor — patents filed: FR2865054 (process for the automated composition of a symbolic pattern — direct forerunner of the method of La Livrée d'Hermès), FR2840678 (convertible weapon for martial arts).
