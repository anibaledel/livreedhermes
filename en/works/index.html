Preprints and notes

  • Strict Log-Concavity Across All Scales for Exact-Height Plane Binary Trees

    Working preprint: for every sufficiently large height h, the coefficient column counting plane binary trees of exact height h is strictly log-concave on its whole interior. Confirmed by exact computation up to h = 16; not yet read by a specialist.

    doi:10.5281/zenodo.23102264 (all versions, to cite) Preprint · version 1.0.0, October 2, 2026 · DOI of this version · CC BY 4.0
  • Sign-Quotiented Doubling Modulo n: Cycle Structure, 2-Adic Synchronization, Lifting, and Forward-Closed Subsets — Four Working Papers

    Four working papers on multiplication by 2 in Z/nZ after identifying x with −x, for every n: cycle structure, 2-adic synchronization, 2-adic lifting, and the enumeration of subsets closed under doubling. This doubling generates the vocabulary of axes of the book (n = 12).

    doi:10.5281/zenodo.23088601 (all versions, to cite) Preprint · version 1.1.1, October 2, 2026 · DOI of this version · CC BY 4.0
  • One Object, Three Descriptions: Axes, Images and Grids of a Jacquard Pattern System

    The three descriptions of the pattern system (axes, loom images, level sets of a square-plate eigenmode) present one finite structure, checked exhaustively on the sixty published images.

    doi:10.5281/zenodo.22986529 (all versions, to cite) Preprint · version 1.2.1, October 2, 2026 · DOI of this version · CC BY 4.0
  • Axes and Level Sets of a Jacquard Pattern System — two companion notes

    The vocabulary of axes generated by an inverse doubling (note [A]), and its identification with the level sets of a square-plate eigenmode (note [P]).

    doi:10.5281/zenodo.22965031 (all versions, to cite) Preprint · version V.3, October 2, 2026 · first version September 25, 2026 · DOI of this version · CC BY 4.0
  • Parity Figures as Moiré: What Is Exact and What Is Not

    Where the parity rule sits in the theory of moiré, and what becomes false when it is stated too quickly.

    doi:10.5281/zenodo.22967584 (all versions, to cite) Preprint · version 1.0.1, September 27, 2026 · first version September 25, 2026 · DOI of this version · CC BY 4.0
