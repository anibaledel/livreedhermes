See your music

Every piece of music has a signature: the way its energy is spread across frequencies. Choose a track, a file or the microphone: the page measures that energy at the frequencies of each circle of modes of a square plate, then looks, among the unified patterns of La Livrée d'Hermès, for the one whose spectrum resembles it most. The choice rests on the resemblance of two spectra, not on a musical law: a pattern does not “express” a piece.

Nothing leaves your browser: the file, the microphone and the captured audio are analysed on the spot and sent nowhere.

Warning: the images can change fast and flicker. The “reduce rapid changes” setting slows them down.
