FRAME

ANIBAL

AMIOT

"Duration is a state in which movement is not annihilated by obstacles. It is not a state of rest, for pure immobility is regression. Rather, duration is a movement unfolding according to determined laws, closed in on itself and, consequently, constantly renewing itself, of an organized whole, strongly centered on itself, in which every end is followed by a new beginning. The end is reached through inward movement, the inhalation of breath, systole, concentration. This movement is transformed into a new beginning in which it is directed outward: this is the exhalation of breath, diastole, expansion. It is in this way that celestial bodies complete their course in the sky and can therefore shine in a lasting manner. The seasons unfold according to a fixed law of change and transformation and can consequently work enduringly. Thus, the person who has heeded the call embodies lasting meaning in their way of life, and the world receives, through this, a form." "From that in which things derive their duration, it is possible to recognize the nature of all beings in heaven and on Earth." Excerpt from Confucius' commentary on hexagram 32, HONG / DURATION

BOOK OF CHANGES or Yi-King and Serpent Mosaic.

Yi means chameleon and KING refers to the weaving of the cloth in which the sacred books were wrapped. Thus, the word Yi-King literally translates as "The Weave of the Chameleon Cloth" or Book of Changes.

AXES OF SYMMETRY

Each set of axes is associated with a type of symmetry according to this protocol:

YING, YANG, YANG MUT, or YING MUT.

These four types of bases can be combined. Combining all four bases yields 1 set. Combining three bases yields 4 sets. Combining two bases yields 6 sets.

CORRESPONDING PATTERNS

Each of these 15 natures generates image checkerboards based on a corresponding image and its antipode (an image inverting the three hues). The set of three-hue images is composed like the hexagrams of six levels. Each level of the image is associated with a line of the hexagram. The superposition of the six yang levels produces the presented yang image, the superposition of the six ying levels produces the corresponding ying image, and the blending of these 12 levels or layers generates an image checkerboard.
