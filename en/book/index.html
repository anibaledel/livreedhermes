La Livrée d'Hermès

La Livrée d'Hermès is a book of philosophy and mathematics, freely available online. It is centered on the construction of magic squares and their transcription into Jacquard weaving — a treatise in arithmogeometry that carries the reader from the magic square to the loom itself.
