Documentary profile

Patents and registered designs, verifiable with INPI

Portrait — photographic montage

Anibal Amiot, also known by his full name Anibal Edelberto Amiot, is an author, publisher, designer, artist and inventor. His book-publishing activity was carried out notably through Agnibal Editions (SIREN 841620818, Paris), registered under NAF code 5811Z, book publishing. He is the author of La Livrée d'Hermès, a book of arithmogeometry freely available on this site, and the holder of the patents and registered designs detailed below, filed in France and verifiable with the French National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI). His Wikidata entry lists his activities and his INPI filings.

Patents

Patent FR2840678 — Arme transformable pouvant convenir à la pratique de plusieurs arts martiaux (convertible weapon suited to the practice of several martial arts)

Filing (priority): FR0206929, June 5, 2002
Publication: FR2840678A1, December 12, 2003
Grant: FR2840678B1, September 22, 2006

Patent FR2865054 — Procédé et dispositif pour la composition automatisée d'un motif symbolique exprimant une relation mathématique (process and device for the automated composition of a symbolic pattern expressing a mathematical relation)

Filing: FR0400189, January 9, 2004
Publication: FR2865054A1, July 15, 2005
Grant: FR2865054B1, July 4, 2008

This second patent, on the automated composition of a symbolic pattern from a mathematical relation, is the direct forerunner of the system of layers developed in La Livrée d'Hermès.

Registered designs

63 designs registered on July 4, 2019 under registration number 20193057, covering the principal order-12 squares — the geometric foundation of La Livrée d'Hermès.

Verifiable sources: data.inpi.fr, Google Patents, Espacenet.
