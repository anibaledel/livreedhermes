About

Arithmogeometry is the construction of magic squares by a geometric rather than arithmetic method: each number is laid down as a figure, derived from the ansate cross, and the whole transcribes directly into Jacquard weave. The term is proposed by Anibal Edelberto Amiot in La Livrée d'Hermès (2026), cited by its DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22722485; the author is identified by Wikidata and ORCID.

The articles are available in English, as are the presentation of the author and his research deposits. The interactive tools are in French.
