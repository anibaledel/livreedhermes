Résumé

Data and verification scripts accompanying the note A Half-Shift Criterion on Three-Colour 12 × 12 Grids, and Its Restriction on the Cube, submitted to the Journal of Mathematics and the Arts.

The note studies a finite set of three-colour 12 × 12 grids arising from a Jacquard weaving system, and the single criterion imposed on them: translating a grid by half its period must reproduce the grid obtained by inverting its two generating layers. These scripts reproduce every numerical statement in the text.

How to use it. The deposit is a single archive. Unpack it keeping its directory structure: the scripts are in tools/, the data in data/, and cube_edges.py looks for its reference files in the data/ directory beside its own. Run the scripts from tools/. Flattened, they stop with "données introuvables".

half-shift-cube-v3/
tools/ the eight scripts
data/ the three reference files
logs/ the output of each script
fig/ the five figures of the note
CHANGES.md
SHA256SUMS.txt

The scripts.

cube_edges.py — rebuilds the corpus from the layer data, checks the stated hypotheses and reproduces the counts of Sections 4, 6 and 8: 1024 triplets, 512 distinct grids, 256 tilings; the vertex condition; the edge rule with its 512 / 256 / 128; the local and global obstructions; the duplication lemma; and the orbit counts. It is the shared library: croisements.py, cube_croisements.py and paires_croisees.py depend on it.

croisements.py — enumerates the 1770 layer pairs and the 113,280 constructions, counts those that stand alone, takes quotients by the symmetry group, and reproduces the classification of Section 7.

cube_croisements.py and paires_croisees.py — the long forms of the above, kept for cross-checking: same counts, reported step by step.

selection_ordre6.py — reproduces Section 8: the 256 order-6 squares, their grouping into 64 order-12 forms, and the 16 satisfying the criterion.

croix_ansee.py — enumerates the stroke configurations on the 6 × 6 grid: 64 raw choices, 8 coherent pairings, exactly 2 admissible.

verif_carre_magique.py — checks a given order-6 square: magic sums, associativity, and the four colour conditions. Standalone.

make_figures.py — regenerates the five figures of the note from the same data. Standalone.

The data.

referent_256_v3.json — the 256 order-6 squares, with per-colour positions and chirality;

referent_360_v3.json — the 360 layers by family, nature and level: 15 families, 60 layers;

fonds_ecran_v1.json — the level map and the 60 layers.

The outputs. logs/ holds the timestamped output of each of the eight scripts, so a result can be read rather than recomputed; and if it is recomputed, the comparison covers every line except the last, which reports elapsed time and depends on the machine.

Checksums. SHA256SUMS.txt lists every file in the archive except itself. Check with sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt from the root of the unpacked archive.

Measured running times, for guidance: cube_edges.py about 55 s, cube_croisements.py 43 s, croisements.py 30 s, paires_croisees.py 13 s, selection_ordre6.py 1 s, the other three under a second.

Change in version 3. Version 2 contained only cube_edges.py, its log and CHANGES.md: a Zenodo version does not inherit from the previous one, so the scripts and reference files announced by the description were absent. Version 3 restores the full set, adds the output logs, the five figures and the checksums; version 3.2 deposits them as an archive so that the directory structure survives the download.
