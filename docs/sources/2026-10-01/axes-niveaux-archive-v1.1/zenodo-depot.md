## Description

À coller tel quel (Zenodo accepte le HTML ; les balises ci-dessous sont les
seules utilisées).

```html
<p>Two companion notes on one finite object: a vocabulary of straight lines on a
12 × 12 square, and the identification of that vocabulary with the level sets of
an eigenmode of a square plate.</p>

<p><strong>[A] A Vocabulary of Axes Generated by an Inverse Doubling.</strong>
The construction's vocabulary is shown to be generated rather than chosen. One
operation suffices — doubling a line's offset about the centre — and the origin
is forced: a level fixed by the doubling has no preimage outside itself, so it
cannot be generated and must be posited, while every other level is reachable.
There are exactly two such levels, 0° and 120°, the second existing precisely
because 3 divides 12. From those two the orthogonal vocabulary closes in two
steps on 24 offsets and the diagonal one in three, the latter including the
inscribed diamond, which belongs to the vocabulary and is not drawn. The
backward structure is two towers of height two rather than a single chain; the
parity rule that turns a set of axes into a figure is injective on band systems;
the integrity check the construction affords is a linear code, [1152, 13] of
minimum distance 288 on the triangle reading; and the period 12 is itself
constrained, the descent terminating without a cycle exactly for N = 2^a or
3·2^a. A separate result, checked on all sixty published images, identifies the
fifteen families of an existing pattern system with the fifteen non-empty unions
of four families of the first generation.</p>

<p><strong>[P] A Generative Construction of the Level-Set Family of a
Square-Plate Eigenmode.</strong> Twelve of the sixteen axis families are
complete level sets of the single function cos(πx/3) at the seven levels cos θ,
θ = 0°, 30°, …, 180°; the construction's involution is exactly the reflection
θ ↦ 180° − θ. That function is an exact eigenfunction of the Kirchhoff plate
with all four edges guided, and the degenerate combinations cos(4πx/a) ±
cos(4πy/a) are exact guided modes whose nodal sets are two of the diagonal
families — one of which is the 48-cell skeleton that all sixty images of the
independent corpus carry in common. The same function is also the exact
eigenfunction of a second, trivially realisable system, the acoustic pressure in
a rigid-walled rectangular cavity, where the governing equation is Helmholtz
rather than biharmonic: the two share their eigenfunctions and not their
spectra, so a single ratio of measured frequencies discriminates between them.
One falsifiable interferometric prediction is stated. No physical measurement
was performed.</p>

<p><strong>Contents.</strong> Both notes as PDF and Markdown source; the axis
catalogue as JSON; eight scripts that print every number cited, with the output
of their last run; the two figures of [A] as SVG, generated by one of those
scripts from the same catalogue.</p>

<p><strong>Licences.</strong> Texts and figures: CC BY 4.0. Code: AGPL v3.</p>
```

## Notes additionnelles

```
A convention must be named before the code parameters are quoted. The parity of
a figure is defined up to a reference region. On the triangle reading (C8) the
parameters do not depend on it: [1152, 13], minimum distance 288, a unique
minimum-weight word. On the cell reading (C1) the minimum distance is 36 in
every case, but the dimension is 12 if figures carry the polarity the rule gives
them and 11 if a reference region is required to be light, because the all-dark
figure lies in the span on C1 and not on C8. The name of the minimum-weight word
likewise depends on the reference: T0 YANG MUT + T1 YIN from the corner region,
T0 YANG MUT + T1 YIN MUT from the central one. See [A] §5.4.
```
