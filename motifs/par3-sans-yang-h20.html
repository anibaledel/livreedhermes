Unified Pattern — Triple — without YANG, hexagram 20 (Jian, Obstruction)

One of 256 distinct Unified Patterns — a 12×12 Jacquard construction where every cell's colour is read off a single hexagram of the Yi King, hexagram Jian, Obstruction.

Family Triple — without YANG, one of the 8 admissible families (out of 15) in the system's classification — see the lexicon for how families are built and admitted.

Reading the cell, line by line

The 144 cells of the 12×12 cell fall into six levels of 24 cells, one per line of the hexagram. Where the line is solid (yang), the level takes its colours from the family's YANG grid; where it is broken (yin), from its YANG mutant grid. Count of each colour, level by level:

The three colours are exactly balanced: 48 cells each. The cell is unchanged by reflection in the diagonal, a half turn.

Hexagram 20 — Obstruction

Upper trigram ☵ Kan (The Abysmal, Water); lower trigram ☶ Gen (Keeping Still, the Mountain).

Image

On the mountain, Water: the image of obstruction. Water meets the slope and cannot advance directly — it must go around, seek another path, slow its natural course. This is the figure of difficulty that imposes a detour. Thus the noble one turns back upon himself and shapes his own virtue: when the outer obstacle cannot be forced, it becomes the occasion to work on what, within, can still grow.

Judgement — Restraint

Patience itself becomes a shield against the terrain. Facing what cannot be crossed head-on, patience is not resignation but an active protection — it preserves energy for the moment when the passage will open. This line says every obstacle holds, in outline, the indication of the right detour.

The full hexagram page: the six lines, its magic square and the other patterns built on it.

Same shape, other colourings

Two involutions leave this motif's shape unchanged and only permute its three tints — verified across every family and every hexagram in the corpus, not just this one: swapping the pairing used to read the hexagram (YANG/YANG-mutant ↔ YIN/YIN-mutant), and taking the hexagram's binary complement (63 − 20 = 43). Toggle them below; the tiling never changes, only the colours do.
